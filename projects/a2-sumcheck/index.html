Convincing someone of a sum they never add up

A prover adds together thousands of numbers and states the total. The verifier never sees more than one of them, and still catches a lie with near-certainty. This is the sumcheck protocol, the engine inside GKR and most of today's proof systems. Press Next step and watch it run.

Can a liar get through?

A cheating prover survives a round only if the verifier's random challenge happens to land on a root of the difference of two degree-1 polynomials. With v rounds that is at most d·v / p. Run it and count.
