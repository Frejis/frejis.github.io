Infrastructure Pivot

When a fraud campaign is taken down, the same people are usually running dozens of other servers that look unconnected. Investigators find them by noticing small technical details two machines have in common - the same security certificate, the same login key, the same icon in the browser tab - because details like that are set up once and copied. The trap is that some of those details are shared by half the internet, and treating one of those as a lead buries you in servers that have nothing to do with each other. Every host, address and organisation on this page is invented.

Each dot is a host. A line means two hosts share at least one fingerprint below a near-universal threshold. Highlighted nodes are the current selection.

A tight highlighted cluster is a claim you could defend: these machines were set up by the same hand. A highlight sprawling across the whole canvas is the same click producing no claim at all, which is why the score matters more than the count.
